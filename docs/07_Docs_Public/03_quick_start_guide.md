# 03. Quick Start Guide: The Basics 🚀📸

**Version:** 1.0.27 | **Author:** Gerard (`fotologue@pm.me` | `https://www.fotologue.au/`)

---

## 5W's Strategic Overview
* **WHO**: New Fotologue users opening the app for the first time.
* **WHAT**: A lightweight, progressive onboarding guide to get shooting in under 2 minutes.
* **WHY**: Avoid the disappointment of good intentions and bad habits. On-the-spot simple cataloguing.
* **WHEN**: First launch / initial setup.
* **HOW**: Progressive disclosure—how far you go is entirely up to you.

---

## 1. Core Philosophy: On-the-Spot Simple Cataloguing

Paper notebooks get lost, fumbled in the field, or abandoned. Fotologue connects all the planning, places, and pieces that go into every frame—the exact camera body, lens, film stock, filter, GPS location, and metering data—into a single controlled record so you can learn from your work and refine your craft over time.

> **Rule #1: How far you go is up to you!**  
> You can use Fotologue as a 1-tap fast field log on street shoots, or engage deep 3-axis tagging and darkroom ledgers for archival publication projects.

---

## 2. Step 1: Add Your Camera & Lens (1 Minute)

1. Open **`Cameras, Lenses, Films & Accessories`** from the main staging portal.
2. Tap **`Cameras`** $\to$ tap the **`+`** button.
3. Type your camera model (*e.g., Canon AE-1, Pentax LX, Leica M6, Hasselblad 503CX*).
4. Tap **`🌐 Search Online Databases`** to pre-fill specs (mount, shutter speeds, format) in 1 tap, or select from the local catalog presets.
5. Tap **`Save Camera`**.

---

## 3. Step 2: Log Your First Exposure in the Field (30 Seconds)

1. Tap **`Exposure Logging & Classification`** $\to$ **`Log Exposure`** (or tap the **Home Screen Widget**).
2. Select your active Camera, Lens, and Film Roll (*e.g. Roll 1.01*).
3. Select your **Aperture** (*f/5.6*) and **Shutter Speed** (*1/250s*).
4. GPS location and timestamp are captured **automatically in the background**.
5. Tap **`Save Exposure Log`**!

---
