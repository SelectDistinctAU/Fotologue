## Full Description (Google Play & Apple App Store)

**Fotologue: Professional Exposure Log, Light Meter & Darkroom Lab**

Elevate your film photography workflow with Fotologue, the ultimate cross-platform companion for analogue photographers, Directors of Photography (DPs), and wet darkroom craftspeople. Designed for precision, speed, and 100% offline privacy, Fotologue simplifies logging exposure data, managing camera gear, metering light, tracking chemical stock, and exporting XMP sidecars for desktop DAMs.

**Key Features:**

* 📷 **10,000+ Master Gear Presets & Camera-wiki.org Integration:** Instant 1-tap setup covering iconic camera bodies (*Canon T50, T70, T80, T90, AE-1, F-1, Pentax LX, Leica M6, Hasselblad 503CX, Yashica Mat-124G*) and lenses. Live online search queries **Camera-wiki.org MediaWiki API** (`{{Camera}}` infobox templates) and **Wikidata API** for 100% accurate spec resolution!
* 🎯 **5-Spot OM-4 Multi-Spot Metering & Weighted Averaging:** Modeled after the legendary Olympus OM-4, sample up to 5 scene points on the live viewfinder feed. Tapping shadow or highlight areas multiple times weights them in the final Average EV calculation!
* 📊 **Ansel Adams 11-Zone Live Histogram & Spatial Zebras:** Real-time pixel distribution across Zones 0 through X. Red zebra stripes highlight overexposure (Zone X) and blue zebra stripes highlight crushed shadows (Zone 0) ONLY over specific physical frame regions.
* 🔒 **Single Master Picker Cell & Parameter Locking:** Long-press Aperture (`f/4.0`) or Shutter Speed (`1/250s`) on the master cell row to lock it (`🔒`). Shifting the unlocked parameter dynamically shifts the live Zonal Histogram and zebra clipping mask in real time!
* 🧪 **Master Inventory Barcode Auto-Fill:** 1-tap UPC/EAN barcode scanner in `Add Inventory Item`. Scanning product barcodes (*e.g., UPC `019498905162` for Ilford Ilfosol 3 500ml*) auto-fills Category, Manufacturer, Item Name, and Container Units in 1 tap!
* 🌙 **TopBar Safelight Moon Toggle:** 1-tap TopBar **Moon IconButton** (`R.drawable.night_sight_max_24px`) activates red-shift night vision mode in complete darkness.
* 🏷️ **3-Axis Tagging System (Subject • Content • Intent):** Organise your work with structured 3-axis tags (*Subject > Architecture, Intent > Series > Urban Decay*) for professional library management and book publication projects.
* 🎞️ **Digital Contact Sheet & Printable PDF Proof Sheets:** Interactive roll grid view with in-grid gap adjustment (+1 / -1 offset) and exportable A4 PDF contact proof sheets ready for binder archiving.
* 💻 **ISO 16684 (XMP) Sidecars & Scanner Matching:** Auto-names exported `.xmp` sidecars (*e.g. `_DSF1024.xmp`*) to match RAW scan file sequences from Fujifilm, Canon, Nikon, Sony, Leica, or flatbed scanners for seamless import into Lightroom, Capture One, digiKam, or darktable.
* 🔒 **100% Local-First Privacy:** No accounts, no cloud database tracking, no subscription fees, and zero user telemetry. All shooting data and location logs stay 100% on your device.
