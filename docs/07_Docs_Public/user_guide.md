## 1. Core Concepts & App Architecture

Fotologue is structured into **8 focused, non-linear modules** accessible directly from the main Staging Portal:

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                       FOTOLOGUE MAIN STAGING PORTAL                          │
├──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 1. [ Cameras, Lenses, Films & Accessories ]  -> Camera Gear Library          │
│ 2. [ Assets & Inventory ]                    -> Physical Stock & Inbound     │
│ 3. [ Exposure Logging & Classification ]    -> Field Shooting & Light Meter  │
│ 4. [ Subject, Content and Intent Tags ]      -> 3-Axis Tagging & Indexing     │
│ 5. [ People & Vendor Management ]            -> Contacts, Labs & Techs        │
│ 6. [ Darkroom Processing & Printing ]        -> Film Developing & Enlarger    │
│ 7. [ Studio Analysis & Reflection ]          -> Contact Sheets & Proofs       │
│ 8. [ Archive Vault ]                         -> Backups, CSV & XMP Sidecars   │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

## 2. Level 1: Getting Started — Your Camera Gear Library

Before logging exposures, configure your photographic equipment in **Cameras, Lenses, Films & Accessories**:

* **Master Preset Catalog**: Access over 80+ classic film camera bodies (*Nikon F3, Canon AE-1, Pentax LX, Leica M6, Hasselblad 503CX, Yashica Mat-124G*), 26 lenses, and film stock emulsions.
* **1-Tap Web Spec Search (`WebSpecSearchEngine`)**: Search for any unlisted gear (*e.g., HD PENTAX-FA 43mm f/1.9 Limited*) to automatically fetch technical specs (focal length, apertures, filter thread size, mount) and pre-fill your entry in 1 tap.
* **1-Tap Interactive Availability Status**: Tap the status badge on any camera or lens card to switch between **`In Stock & Available`**, **`In Transit`**, **`On Loan`**, **`In Repair / CLA`**, or **`Sold / Archived`**.

## 3. Level 2: Film Photographers & Commercial Print Lab Users

If you shoot film and send rolls to a commercial lab (*e.g., Fitzgerald Photo, Silver Salt, Rewind Lab, B&H*):

### A. Exposure Logging in the Field (`FOTOLOGUE FIELD`)
* **1-Tap Exposure Logging**: Log shutter speed, aperture, ISO, film roll/frame number (*e.g., Roll 1.01*), GPS geotags, and notes in seconds.
* **Precision Spot Light Meter**: Point your camera at highlights or shadows for real-time target EV calculation, spot or center-weighted metering modes, and automatic ND/contrast filter stop compensation.
* **Home Screen App Widget**: Tap the `[ + ]` or `[ 🥾 ]` widget buttons on your phone's home screen for instant 1-tap exposure logging and spot metering without searching for the app!

### B. Lab Drop-off & Custody Tracking
* **People & Vendor Management**: Maintain a directory of your favorite print labs, phone numbers, and processing instructions.
* **Custody & Loans Ledger**: Track film rolls sent to labs or gear loaned to friends.

## 4. Level 3: Hybrid & Digital Photographers (3-Axis Tags & Desktop Exports)

If you digitize negatives, scan film at home, or manage digital RAW files in **Adobe Lightroom, Capture One, or digiKam**:

### A. 3-Axis Taxonomy Tagging (`Subject, Content and Intent Tags`)
Categorize every exposure across three distinct dimensions:
1. **Subjects**: Domain classification (*Architecture, Landscape, Portrait, Street, Wildlife*).
2. **Content**: Specific visual details (*Bridge, Building, Waterways, Fungi, Skyline*).
3. **Intent**: Purpose of the photo series (*Study, Series, Documentation, Personal, Commission*).

### B. Digital Contact Sheets & Frame Gap Alignment (`STATION`)
* **Visual Fingerprint Comparison**: View full film roll grids with scene thumbnails to match digital RAW scan files against physical film negatives.
* **In-Grid Frame Gap Adjuster**: Tap blank frames to insert or remove frame gaps (+1 / -1 offset) to keep digital contact sheets 1-to-1 aligned with physical film rebate numbers.

### C. Desktop Library Exporters (`ARCHIVE`)
* **ISO 16684 XMP Sidecar Exports**: Generates `.xmp` sidecar files containing 3-Axis tag hierarchies (`lr:hierarchicalSubject`), EXIF exposure parameters, GPS coordinates, and creator copyright.
* **CSV Summary Exports**: Export CSV logs for Excel data analysis or catalog indexing.

## 5. Level 4: Wet Darkroom & Advanced Enthusiasts (`LAB`)

For darkroom printers and photographers developing their own film at home:

### A. Film Development Timer & 60 BPM Agitation Metronome
* **Live Step-by-Step Timer**: Auto-calculates processing times for developer, stop bath, fixer, and wash (*e.g., HP5+ in ID-11, Tri-X in D-76, C-41, E-6*).
* **Acoustic Agitation Metronome**: Zero-latency **60 BPM acoustic metronome** with 3-second pre-warning pips, allowing you to time tank inversions in complete darkness without looking at a screen.
* **Red Shift Night Vision Mode**: 1-tap red shift mode eliminates blue light to preserve night-adapted vision.

### B. Enlarger Printing Session Ledger
* **Enlarger Machine & Paper Stock Specs**: Track enlarger machines, enlarger lenses, lens-to-paper distances, and magnification.
* **Split-Grade Exposure Step Ledgers**: Record base exposure times, contrast grade filters (0–5 in half-stop steps), dodge/burn steps, and dry-down factor compensation.

## 6. Level 5: Studio Assets, Physical Inventory & Inbound Orders

Keep your photography studio, storage, and supplies organized:

* **Consumables Stock Tracking**: Monitor chemical capacity, paper boxes, wetting agents, and safety gear.
* **Storage Locations**: Assign items to dry cabinets, studio shelves, camera bags, and paper safes.
* **Pre-Arrival Inbound Orders**: Track incoming camera or lens shipments. Tapping **`Mark Delivered`** converts the inbound order into an active asset in your library without creating duplicates!

---

## 7. Level 6: Archive Vault, Backups & Desktop Sync

* **Catalogue Backup & Restore**: Export full-fidelity `.json` catalogue backups with non-destructive merge imports.
* **Desktop Sync Manifests**: Exchange equipment profiles, contacts, and storage locations bidirectionally with Fotologue Desktop.
* **Cloud Master Preset Refresh**: Tap **`[ 🔄 Refresh Master Presets from Cloud ]`** to pull live camera and lens catalog updates directly from GitHub without waiting for an app store update!

---

## 8. Privacy & Security

* **100% On-Device Data**: All thumbnails, GPS coordinates, notes, and gear lists remain stored locally on your device.
* **On-Device Machine Learning**: Smart Face-Aware tagging operates entirely on-device; no images or biometric data are ever transmitted or uploaded.
* **Zero Tracking**: Fotologue contains zero third-party analytics, advertisements, or cloud lock-in.
