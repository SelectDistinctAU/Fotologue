## 1. Google Group & Play Store Closed Testing Copy

> **Fotologue** logs exposure data, GPS location, roll and frame numbers, and 3-axis tags (Subject / Content / Intent) against each frame, then exports them as standard XMP sidecars. Everything stays on your device. No accounts, no cloud, no tracking.
> 
> The core philosophy behind Fotologue is an end-to-end, simple, on-the-spot cataloguing and geotagging of analogue images.
> 
> Avoid the disappointment of good intentions and bad habits where you lose track of shot details or end up trying to remember settings weeks later. Fotologue connects all the planning, places and pieces that go into every frame — the exact camera body, lens, film stock, filter, GPS location, and metering data; right through to the darkroom, printing and storage or disposal — into a single controlled record so photographers can learn from their work and refine their craft over time.
> 
> Currently in closed testing on Android (Google Play availability may vary by region). Join this group, then grab the app from Google Play or as a direct download.
> 
> I'm looking for serious film photographers to use it in the field and tell me what breaks or doesn't make sense. Feedback goes to **`fotologue@pm.me`**. If it goes well, I'd like to form a steering group to help shape what comes next.
> 
> **Website**: [www.fotologue.au](https://www.fotologue.au/)

## 2. Testing Boundaries & Feature Freeze Policy

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **FEATURE SET IS FROZEN FOR V1.0 RELEASE**
> We are strictly hunting for:
> 1. **Broken Features & Crashes**: Anything that force-closes or fails to save.
> 2. **Confusing UX / UI**: Layouts that don't make sense or obstruct field shooting.
> 3. **Data Integrity Issues**: Incorrect EXIF, missing GPS geotags, or catalog export mismatches.

### How to Report Bugs:
Send an email to **`fotologue@pm.me`** with:
1. **Device Model & Android Version** (e.g. *Pixel 8, Android 15*).
2. **What you were doing when it broke** (e.g. *Tapping 'Save Camera' in preset browser*).
3. **Screenshot or Screen Recording** (if applicable).

## 3. Parking Good Ideas Policy

If you have a brilliant idea for a new feature (e.g. *Bluetooth light meter sync, new export format, AI frame recognition*):
- **We want to hear it!** Send it to `fotologue@pm.me` tagged with `[Idea]`.
- All feature requests will be logged in our **Post-Launch Feature Backlog** and evaluated by the Steering Panel.

If you are an active film photographer using Fotologue in the field and would like to help guide the future roadmap:
