# Fotologue Store Listing Descriptions

**Version:** 1.0.17 (Build 18) | **Release Date:** September 2024 | **Audience:** Play Console Marketing, Localisation & Reviewers

## English (Primary)
**Fotologue: Professional Exposure Log & Light Meter**

Elevate your film photography workflow with Fotologue, the ultimate companion for analogue enthusiasts. Designed for precision and speed, Fotologue simplifies the process of logging exposure data, managing your gear, and metering light, all while keeping your data strictly private.

**Key Features:**

*   **Professional Exposure Logging:** Never forget your settings again. Quickly record shutter speed, aperture, ISO, focal length, and even location for every frame. Add notes and tags to organise your shooting sessions.
*   **Intelligent Logging Suggestions:** Improved "Film Roll.Frame" logging with smart sequential suggestions (e.g., 1.01, 1.02) to keep your workflow fluid.
*   **Integrated Light Meter:** Turn your phone into a high-precision light meter. Choose between Spot metering for specific details or Centre-weighted metering for general scenes. Calibrate the meter to match your camera’s unique behaviour.
*   **Smart Face-Aware Tagging:** Fast, on-device detection that suggests "People" tags while keeping your privacy intact.
*   **3-Axis Tagging System (Subject • Content • Intent):** Organise your work with a structured system (e.g., Subject > Nature, Intent > Series > Urban Decay) for professional-grade library management and book publication projects.
*   **Gear Library Management:** Keep track of your entire arsenal. Manage a detailed database of your Cameras, Lenses, Film stocks, and accessories like Viewfinders, Teleconverters, Filters, and Flashes. Create "Kits" for your favourite combinations to speed up logging in the field.
*   **Professional Metadata & XMP Sidecars:** Store Creator, Copyright, and Contact information directly in Settings. Automatically generate ISO 16684 (XMP) sidecar files for seamless metadata embedding in Lightroom or Capture One.
*   **Camera Macro Copy Presets & Scanner Matching:** Auto-names `.xmp` sidecars to match RAW scan file sequences from Fujifilm, Canon, Nikon, Sony, Leica, or flatbed scanners.
*   **Digital Contact Sheet & Printable PDF Proof Sheets:** Interactive roll grid view with in-grid gap insertion/removal (+1 / -1 offset) and exportable A4 PDF contact proof sheets.
*   **Advanced Export Options:** Your data, your way. Export your logs as CSV, JSON, XMP, or generate a full ZIP package that includes thumbnails and location data for archiving.
*   **Privacy-First Design:** Your photography is personal. Fotologue stores all data locally on your device. No accounts, no cloud syncing, and no tracking. You are in full control of your information.
*   **Modern Material You Interface:** Experience a fast, intuitive UI that looks great and supports landscape mode for a better view on any device.

Whether you're a seasoned professional or just starting your journey into analog photography, Fotologue provides the tools you need to master your exposures and document your craft.

Download Fotologue today and focus on what matters most: the art of film.
