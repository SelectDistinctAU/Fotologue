## Full Description (Google Play & Apple App Store)

**Fotologue: Professional Exposure Log, Light Meter & Darkroom Lab**

Elevate your film photography workflow with Fotologue, the ultimate cross-platform companion for analogue photographers, Directors of Photography (DPs), and wet darkroom craftspeople. Designed for precision, speed, and 100% offline privacy, Fotologue simplifies logging exposure data, managing camera gear, metering light, tracking chemical stock, and exporting XMP sidecars for desktop DAMs.

**Key Features:**

* 📷 **115+ Master Gear Presets & Brand-First Cascading:** Instant 1-tap setup covering 82 iconic camera bodies (Nikon F5, Leica M6 TTL, Canonet QL17, Rolleiflex 2.8F, Mamiya RZ67) and 93 master lenses (Canon L-Series, Nikkor Ai-S/ED, Pentax SMC, Olympus Zuiko, Leica Summilux).
* 🔄 **1-Click Cloud Preset Refreshing:** Tap "Refresh Master Presets" in Settings to fetch the latest camera, lens, and film catalog updates live over HTTPS without waiting for an App Store release!
* ☀️ **Calibrated Pro Light Meter & Filter Compensation:** Turn your phone into a spot or centre-weighted light meter with live C-41, E-6, and B&W pre-visualization. Automatic stop compensation calculation when selecting ND filters (*Hoya ND8* +3 stops), polarizers (*CPL* +1.5 stops), or B&W contrast filters (*Yellow K2* +1 stop).
* 🏷️ **3-Axis Tagging System (Subject • Content • Intent):** Organise your work with structured 3-axis tags (e.g. Subject > Nature, Intent > Series > Urban Decay) for professional-grade library management and book publication projects.
* 📞 **Contacts Directory & Vendor Presets:** 1-click import top international and Australian suppliers & processing labs (*B&H Photo Video, Adorama, Analogue Wonderland, Camera Electronic Perth, Leederville Cameras, Fitzgerald Photo, Rewind Photo Lab, Vanbar, Walkens, Hillvale*).
* 🧪 **Wet Darkroom Inventory & Equipment:** 30 Darkroom Chemistry & Paper Presets (*Kodak D-76, Xtol, HC-110, Ilford ID-11, DD-X, Rapid Fixer, Multigrade Papers*) and 37 Lab Hardware Presets (*Beseler 23C III, Durst M605/L1200, LPL 7700, Paterson Super System 4, Jobo 1520/2520, GraLab 300, Peak 10x Grain Scope*).
* 🎞️ **Digital Contact Sheet & Printable PDF Proof Sheets:** Interactive roll grid view with in-grid gap adjustment (+1 / -1 offset) and exportable A4 PDF contact proof sheets ready for binder archiving.
* 💻 **ISO 16684 (XMP) Sidecars & Scanner Matching:** Auto-names exported `.xmp` sidecars (*e.g. `_DSF1024.xmp`*) to match RAW scan file sequences from Fujifilm, Canon, Nikon, Sony, Leica, or flatbed scanners for seamless import into Lightroom, Capture One, digiKam, or darktable.
* 🔒 **100% Local-First Privacy:** No accounts, no cloud database tracking, no subscription fees, and zero user telemetry. All shooting data and location logs stay 100% on your device.

Whether you're a seasoned professional shooting commercial film sets or an enthusiast printing in the darkroom, Fotologue provides the tools you need to master your exposures and preserve your craft.
